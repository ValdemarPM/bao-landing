## Getting Started

### Overview
Banners All Over is a powerful Shopify app that allows you to create stunning, professional banners to enhance your e-commerce store and convert visitors into customers.

### Main Navigation
When you enter the app, you'll find six main menu options at the top:
- **Banners** - The main section where you create and manage your banners
- **Styling** - Customize the general appearance of your banners
- **Plan** - View and manage your subscription plan
- **Settings** - Configure app preferences
- **Feedback** - Share your thoughts and suggestions
- **Support** - Access help and documentation

---

## Main Dashboard

### Banner Management Section
The main dashboard provides quick access to all banner functions:

1. **Create New Banner Options**
   - New Slide Banner
   - New Sticky Banner

2. **Banner List View**
   - Preview of all created banners
   - Filter options:
     - By type (Slide/Sticky)
     - By status (Published/Unpublished)
   - Search field for finding specific banners

3. **Banner Actions**
   Each banner in the list includes:
    - **Published/Unpublished label** - Indication of the publish status of the banner
   - **Preview images** - View a preview of your banner or slides
   - **Edit button** - Modify banner content and settings
   - **Duplicate button** - Create a copy of the banner
   - **Delete button** - Remove the banner

4. **Expanded Preview**
   Click to expand any banner preview to see:
   - Detailed preview of each slide
   - Active status indicator
   - Scheduling information (start and end dates if applicable)

## Creating and Managing Banners

### Banner Editor Interface

When editing a banner, you'll see:

1. **Header Information - left side**
   - Banner title
   - Auto-save indicator (changes save automatically)
   - Last edited timestamp

2. **Header publishing - right side**
   - **Publish/Unpublish button** - Control banner visibility on your store

3. **Status output bar**
   - A bar where the information about the status of your banner and useful instructions are displyed.

4. **Preview Navigation**
   - **Preview modes** - Test your banner in different responsive views:
     - Large mode (Desktop)
     - Medium mode (Tablet)
     - Small mode (Mobile)
   - Use arrow buttons (left/right) to navigate between slides
   - Navigation dots at the bottom for quick access to specific slides

## Slide Types

### Creating a New Slide
When adding a new slide, choose from four types:

#### 1. Image Only Slide
- Upload three image versions for responsive display
- Add slide title
- Set destination link
- Add an Alt text for the images

#### 2. Image and Text Slide
- Upload an image
   - Add an Alt text for the images
- Add customizable text elements:
  - Title text
  - Description text
  - Call-to-action button text
- Customize styling:
  - Text colors
  - Background colors

#### 3. Video Slide
- Two video source options:
  - External link (YouTube, Vimeo, etc.)
  - Direct video upload
- Display options:
  - Show/hide pause and play buttons
  - Control settings configuration

#### 4. Product Slide
- Search by product title
- Automatically pulls:
  - Product main image
  - Product title
  - Product link
- Customization options:
  - Override product title
  - Add secondary text
  - Show/hide product price
  - Define background
  - Control out-of-stock display

### Slide Management
- **Reorder** - Drag and drop slides to rearrange
- **Hide/Show** - Temporarily disable slides without deleting
- **Duplicate** - Copy a slide for quick creation
- **Delete** - Permanently remove a slide

## Banner Settings

### Scheduler
Configure when your banner appears:
- **Permanent visibility** - Always visible (default)
- **Date range** - Set specific start and end dates/times
  - Start date: Day/Month/Year and Hour
  - End date: Day/Month/Year and Hour

### Display Options
Customize banner appearance:
- Animation type selection
- Animation speed control
- Show/hide navigation arrows
- Show/hide navigation dots

### Playback options
Control banner interaction:
- **Auto-play** - Enable/disable automatic slide progression
- **Pause on hover** - Stop animation when mouse hovers over banner
- **Loop** - Continuous playback of slides
- **Interval time** - Duration each slide is displayed

## Banner Placement

### Section Selection
Choose where your banner appears on your store:
- Homepage
- Collections pages
- Product pages
- Cart page

### Target Positioning
1. Click the **Target** button
2. Navigate to your live store page
3. Hover over different page regions to see placement previews
4. Click to select desired position
5. Accept placement to return to settings

### Display Rules
Set conditions for banner visibility:
- Show only for products with specific rules
- Filter by tags
- Filter by brands/vendors
- Filter by collection
- Filter by product type

### Preview Your Placement
Use the preview button to see your banner in its actual position on your live website before publishing.

## Quick Start Guide

### Get started in 4 simple steps:

1. **Click the New Banner button**
   - Choose between Slide Banner or Sticky Banner

2. **Place the banner in your store**
   - Select target location
   - Position your banner precisely

3. **Add slides**
   - Add images, text, videos, or products
   - Customize each slide's content

4. **Publish your banner**
   - Review your banner
   - Click Publish to make it live on your store
